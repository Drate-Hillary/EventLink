# EventLink User Manual

EventLink helps customers find event-service providers, send quote or booking requests, and keep conversations in one place. Providers use it to present their services and respond to customers. Administrators review providers and help keep the marketplace safe.

## 1. Create an account or sign in

1. Select **Create account**.
2. Choose **Plan an event** to use EventLink as a customer, or **Offer services** to register as a provider.
3. Enter your name, email address, and a password of at least six characters. Providers also enter a business name and service category.
4. If EventLink asks you to confirm your email, follow the link in the confirmation email, then sign in.

Administrators use an administrator account provided by the EventLink team; administrator registration is not available on the public sign-up form.

## 2. For customers

### Plan an event

Open **My events** and select **Create event**. Enter the event name, type, date, location, guest count, total budget, and services you need. Save the event to reuse its details in requests. You can edit or delete an event later.

### Find and compare providers

Open **Find providers** or select **Find vendors** from the main site. Search by business name, service, and location. On the full search page, you can also filter by starting price, minimum rating, verified status, or open dates, and sort by rating, reviews, or price. Select a provider to view its packages, portfolio, reviews, and contact details.

### Send a quote or booking request

On a provider’s profile, select **Request a quote or booking** or choose a package.

- **Ask for a quote** requests pricing information.
- **Request a booking** asks the provider to accept your event date. A request is not confirmed until the provider accepts it.

Choose the event and package, then include any useful details in your message—for example, dietary needs, venue access, or setup requirements. Customers need to create an event before sending a request from their account.

You can also submit a request as a guest by entering your name, email, phone, and location. A guest booking request can be linked to an account from the follow-up prompt; use the same email address so you can track the request and chat with the provider. A guest receipt opens as an email draft and is **not sent automatically**.

### Track requests and chat

Open **Requests** to filter your requests by **All**, **Pending**, **Accepted**, or **Declined**. Open chat from an accepted booking or from the provider’s profile. Messages appear in **Messages**; select a conversation to read or reply. The dashboard also shows unread-message counts and notifications.

### Manage your budget

Open **Budget** and choose an event. Set an amount for each service using the number fields or sliders. The summary shows your allocations and warns you if they exceed your event budget. Adjust the amounts as needed; the planner does not make or collect payments.

## 3. For providers

### Complete your provider profile

After registration, your provider account is **pending** while the EventLink team reviews it. Pending providers are not shown in public vendor search. Once verified, customers can find your listing.

In the provider workspace:

- **Business profile:** update your business name, category, contact details, service locations, and description. Use **Preview public page** to review the listing.
- **Services and packages:** add packages with a name, price in UGX, description, and included services. Edit or remove packages as your offering changes.
- **Portfolio:** add photos with captions, reorder them, or remove them.
- **Availability:** select dates on the calendar to mark them unavailable. Select a marked date again to make it available.

### Respond to customers

Open **Booking requests** to review the customer’s message, package, and event date. Select **View details**, then **Accept** or **Decline**. Acceptance may be blocked if the date is unavailable, conflicts with another accepted booking, or your free-booking allowance has been used.

When a booking is accepted, EventLink creates a conversation for an account-linked customer. Select **Message customer** or open **Enquiries** to continue the conversation. In Enquiries, use **New**, **Awaiting customer**, and **All** to organize conversations.

The first five accepted bookings are free. After that, an active monthly subscription is required to accept more. Subscription payments are made outside EventLink: submit the payment reference under **Subscription and receipts**, then wait for administrator confirmation. Customer booking payments are also arranged directly between the customer and provider; EventLink does not collect them.

Use **Reviews** to read customer feedback and **Performance** to view listing activity and request metrics.

## 4. For administrators

Use the administrator workspace to:

- Review applications in **Provider verification**. Inspect the provider details and submitted documents, then approve, reject, request more information, or suspend as appropriate.
- Search and manage accounts under **Vendors** and **Customers**. Suspended users cannot log in.
- Add, edit, or deactivate marketplace **Service categories**.
- Review all requests under **Requests**. This view is read-only.
- Review reports under **Suspicious activity**. Move a report through its status and record the decision.
- Set the monthly provider subscription price and confirm or reject external payment references under **Billing and revenue**.
- Review administrator actions in **Activity log**.

Some dashboard charts use sample data. Do not treat those charts as authoritative business or financial records.

## 5. Important notes

- Only verified providers appear in public search.
- A booking request is not a confirmed booking until the provider accepts it.
- EventLink does not process customer payments or provider subscription transfers. Agree booking payment directly with the provider, and wait for an administrator to confirm subscription payments.
- If you submitted a guest booking, link it to a customer account to follow its status and use chat.
- Use the **Notifications** control to review updates about requests, messages, and account activity.
